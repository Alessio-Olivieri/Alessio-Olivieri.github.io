01 / INTERPRETABLE AIResearch project

Pruning Vision Transformers through causal analysis

Implemented an ACDC-style approach with activation patching in PyTorch. Identified a sparse subnetwork by pruning 85% of attention heads and 94% of the computational graph, and retrained it to achieve a reported 2× CPU inference speedup with a 9% accuracy drop.

04 / CYBERSECURITY + GRAPH MLResearch ongoing

What can a packet-based model really learn?

Research work with Spognardi on encrypted network traffic classification using ISCX-VPN and graph-based models. Created byte-randomization controls, capture/session provenance grouping, packet-to-model tracing and validation tests. Strong flow-disjoint results exposed an important limitation: insufficient independent captures to establish defensible six-class capture-level generalization.
